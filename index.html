A recreation of the first dungeon from the original 1986 Legend of Zelda: grid-based movement, room-by-room camera transitions, locked doors and keys, every enemy in the Eagle dungeon, and the Aquamentus boss fight.

On top of the recreation, we designed an original seven-room level built around a new parry-and-dash mechanic.

What I built

  • Health, damage, hit-stun and invincibility frames shared by Link and every enemy
  • Room transitions, locked doors and keys, and shutter doors that seal until a room is cleared
  • Stalfos, Keese, Gel, Goriya, Wallmaster, and the Aquamentus boss
  • Authentic enemy and item placement and full dungeon progression
  • The seven-room custom level, armored enemies, and the golden boss

Custom level: Parry & Dash

Parry an attack at the last moment to take no damage. Three parries power up the sword. Dash to slip straight through enemies and hazards. Armored enemies shrug off normal hits, so the player has to choose between standing their ground and getting out of the way.

  1. Parry training. The exit only opens after your first power-up.
  2. Dash training. Armored guards block the only corridor.
  3. Projectiles. Parry Goriya boomerangs to charge up.
  4. Blade gauntlet. Wait for the blades, or dash through them.
  5. Arena. Everything at once for the boss key.
  6. Golden Aquamentus. Only a powered-up sword breaks its armor.
  7. Victory. Claim the Triforce.
